ARoute has two variants: ARoute-Batch operates on a batch of requests known in advance, routing requests with the greatest expected benefit to the stronger model subject to the budget, whereas ARoute-Stream processes requests online, using a threshold calibrated on a small validation set to satisfy the cost quota and dynamically adjusting it as the budget is consumed. For ARoute-Stream, we provide a finite-sample guarantee on expected normalized spending. We evaluate ARoute on MMLU-Pro, LiveCodeBench, SuperGPQA, and two value-filling tasks built from SWAN semantic query benchmark against other state-of-the-art approaches. At matched cost, ARoute-Batch achieves the highest mean accuracy in all 15 task-cost settings, improving over the strongest baseline by 4.1 percentage points on average and by up to 11.9 points. ARoute also completes a batch up to 2.8x faster than cascades.
