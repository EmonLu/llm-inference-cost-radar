To address this, we propose Token Window Pacing (TWP), a closed-loop scheduling framework that leverages runtime feedback from ITL, queue depth, and workload characteristics to regulate request admission at the token level instead of the batch level. TWP dynamically adjusts a selfregulating token window and incorporates chunk-based prefill to minimize interference from incoming requests. Experiments on realistic serverless workloads show that TWP delivers stable TTFT, significantly reduces latency spikes, lowers P99 tail latency, minimizes ITL variation, and improves throughput by up to 45% over ServerlessLLM and 58% over conventional schedulers.
