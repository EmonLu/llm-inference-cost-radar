PatternMiner proves several new theorems related to Schubert positivity and projection, a character-row evaluation formula, a restricted version of the Kazhdan--Lusztig combinatorial invariance conjecture, and Schur positivity for a family of immanant coefficients. We end by analyzing the most common routes to conjecture generation that are used by PatternMiner in our case studies as well as documenting the most common failure modes.
